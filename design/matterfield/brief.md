## New visual system

- Near-black graphite, warm white, silver and a restrained hot-orange accent.
- Archivo display typography and Manrope reading text. Readable labels, no miniature chapter captions.
- The actual NRTH mark in the masthead. Plain navigation, no decorative arrows or pill-shaped CTAs.
- A chrome mineral made from rounded rectangular fragments. The same fragments reorganize into transport paths, a care signal, a systems framework, molecular cells, an event spectrum and a research branching structure.
- Original conceptual architecture and material artwork. None is presented as a photograph of NRTH facilities or evidence of a commercial capability.
- Deep whitespace, oversized editorial statements and a complete venture index. The design's density changes through the journey without turning every section into a card.

## Motion storyboard

1. **Build beyond.** A reflective mineral occupies the right side; pointer movement influences its orientation and fragments. Content is immediately available, with no loader gate.
2. **Nothing great happens in isolation.** The mineral disperses into a spatial field under native scrolling. Reverse scrolling restores the exact sequence.
3. **Find your field.** Six selections reorganize the same objects into meaningful industry forms. Interrupted selections begin at the displayed pose. Keyboard and touch work without pointer hover.
4. **Meet the ventures.** The forms open out as the background moves toward a lighter editorial surface. The nine ventures each have a distinct introduction, focus and connection to the wider portfolio.
5. **A company with room to move.** Company, research, partnership and investor perspectives use the same typography and materials, with new writing.
6. **What comes next?** A direct contact journey closes the narrative. Sending is never claimed unless the configured service accepts the request.

## Concrete quality targets

- Every venture and Investors reachable in no more than two navigation actions.
- One canvas and one shared fragment population; genuine intermediate positions and reversible camera motion, rather than image swaps.
- No sharp jumps on fast scroll, interrupted field selections or reversing direction.
- Native scrolling; no compulsory audio, entry gate, custom hidden cursor or compulsory six-field tour.
- Primary copy and controls fit 360px phones and shorter desktop windows. No horizontal document overflow or pointer-only controls.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, an accessible menu, reduced motion, pause, no JavaScript and graphics failure retain useful content.
- Rendering stops when settled, hidden or outside the scene. Instance count/resolution adapts to smaller devices.
- Browser captures must be compared with the new target and reviewed across opening, intermediate, field, directory and supporting-page states. Repair substantive visual findings before handoff.
- Canonicals, metadata, sitemap, the inquiry service and the protected CMS preview remain usable. No invented customers, launches, certifications, revenue or medical results.
